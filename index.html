Early Blight (Alternaria solani)

94% Confidence
Detected Symptoms:
  • Brown circular concentric target-board lesions
  • Yellowing halos around spots on lower foliage
  • Progressive leaf wilting & stem spot development

Agronomic Action Plan

🚨 Immediate Field Actions:

Prune and burn severely affected lower leaves immediately to prevent fungal spore distribution across neighboring plants.

🧪 Recommended Treatment:

Apply copper-based fungicide or Chlorothalonil at recommended dilution. Organic alternative: Spray neem oil extract (5ml/L) or Bacillus subtilis solution.

🛡️ Preventive Measures:
  • Ensure wider crop spacing (60cm) to promote canopy airflow.
  • Practice 3-year crop rotation with non-solanaceous crops.
  • Use drip irrigation instead of overhead sprinklers.
🚫 Things to Avoid:

Avoid high-nitrogen synthetic fertilizers during active infection as it soft-stimulates vulnerable leaf tissue.
